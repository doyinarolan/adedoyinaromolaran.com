Shape Switch

Use the key to rearrange the shapes. Solve as many as you can before the clock runs out. Every correct answer buys you more time.

How to play

You get three rows: the original order of the shapes, a key of numbers, and the new order.

Each digit in the key points to a position in the original order. Where the digit sits in the key is where that shape goes in the new order.

Key 2 4 3 1: the 1st slot gets shape 2 (Triangle), the 2nd gets shape 4 (Square), the 3rd gets shape 3 (Cross) and the 4th gets shape 1 (Circle).

Drag a tile onto another to swap them, or tap two tiles in turn. Press Check when you’re happy.
